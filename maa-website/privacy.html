Maa for Android

Privacy Policy

How Maa handles your progress, purchases, and support requests.

Last updated 14 November 2026

Learning data

Maa is a Thai typing app developed by Valentin Bell. It does not require a Maa account. The app saves lesson activity in its private Android storage to resume practice, choose what to review, and show your progress. This includes the letters you type in exercises, their timing and accuracy, learned letters and words, session history, and app settings. Maa does not send this progress to a developer-operated server.

Android backup and transfer

Depending on your Android settings, the system may back up parts of your Maa data, including your progress and learning preferences, or transfer them when you set up another device. Your device and backup provider control the backup and restore process. See Android backup and restore to manage your backups.

Pronunciation

Maa sends Thai lesson text to your device’s text-to-speech engine to play pronunciation. Whether the engine uses the network depends on the engine and voice you choose in Android. That engine’s provider has its own privacy practices. Maa does not record your voice.

Purchases

Google Play handles the optional full course purchase. Maa checks purchase status through Google Play and saves signed purchase information on your device so it can verify access when you are offline. Google Play processes payment details. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Maa keeps the local learning data so you can pick up where you left off. To remove it, open Android Settings → Apps → Maa → Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. This removes local progress, saved restore points, and preferences. The in-app Reset progress action keeps a restore point. Manage any Android backups separately. Purchase records held by Google Play are managed through your Google account.

Website and support

The website’s typing example runs in your browser and resets when you reload the page. Maa does not receive your practice input. The site can save your language and appearance choices in your browser for your next visit. The site does not add tracking cookies or analytics. When you visit a site hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs your IP address for security. See the GitHub Pages data collection notice.

If you contact support, Maa uses your email address and the details you provide to understand and respond to your request. Support messages may be kept as needed to resolve and follow up on your request or meet legal obligations. Please leave out payment details and other sensitive information.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions or requests about information you shared with Maa, email the developer at contact@valentinbell.dev.
