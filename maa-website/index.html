From letters
to sentences.

Find the letters

The keyboard starts with blank keys. Tap the gold key to reveal your first letter, then put the letters you know together to spell words and fill in everyday sentences.

Keep the words

Each word you learn goes into your vocabulary list, with its meaning, reading, and examples. Listen when Thai speech is available on your device.

Make the keyboard yours

Discover more characters in the full course to unlock three keyboard styles: Ink, Light, and Jigsaw.

Why I built Maa

The language apps I tried didn’t help me remember the Thai letters. I wanted something I’d use myself, so I built Maa around typing real words, a few letters at a time.

Valentin Bell, developer of Maa
