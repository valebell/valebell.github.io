How to use Maa and what the full course purchase includes.

Last updated 14 November 2026

Using Maa

Maa is an educational app by Valentin Bell for practising Thai letters, words, and phrases. Use the lessons for your own learning and respect the rights of the people who made the course and app. You can start practising without a Maa account.

Free lessons and the full course

The opening lessons and ongoing review of the words and phrases they introduce are free. The remaining guided lessons are available through an optional one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

Buying and restoring the course

Google Play shows the price and handles checkout. The purchase is associated with the Google Play account used at checkout. To restore access after reinstalling or changing Android devices, use the same Google Play account and choose Restore purchase on Maa’s Full course screen. Restoring a purchase restores access to the course. Restoring learning progress depends on Android backup and device transfer.

See Google Play’s refund policy for eligibility and how to request a refund. These terms do not limit any mandatory consumer rights.

Availability

Audio relies on Thai text-to-speech support on your Android device. You can continue practising without sound. Device support and store availability may vary.
