Help with Maa

Support

Find your progress, restore the full course, or get Thai audio working.

How do I restore the full course?

Open Maa’s Full course screen from Settings or Vocabulary. Connect to the internet, confirm that the Google Play Store is using the account that made the purchase, and tap Restore purchase. For refunds or payment problems, see Google Play purchase and refund help.

Why can’t I hear pronunciation?

When Maa reports that Thai audio is unavailable, tap Help and open Speech settings. Install or enable a Thai voice in Android’s text-to-speech settings. Return to Maa and tap Try again. You can keep practising without sound while you set it up.

Will my progress move to a new device?

Maa saves progress on your device. Android may restore it if you have an eligible backup or use a device-to-device transfer. Check your Android backup settings before switching devices. If you reset your progress inside Maa, Settings → Restore previous progress can recover the most recent in-app restore point.

How do I erase my data?

Use Android Settings → Apps → Maa → Storage → Clear storage or Clear data. This removes progress, preferences, and the in-app restore point from that device. See the Privacy Policy for what Android can back up.

Contact Maa

Email the developer at contact@valentinbell.dev. For a bug report, include your Android version, Maa version, and the steps that led to the problem. Leave out passwords and payment information.
